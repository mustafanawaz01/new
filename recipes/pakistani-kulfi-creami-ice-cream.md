# Pakistani Kulfi-Style Ninja CREAMi Ice Cream

## Flavour story

This is a dense, creamy CREAMi kulfi inspired by Pakistani street-style pista kulfi: milky, cooked, cardamom-forward, lightly floral, and nutty rather than vanilla-sweet. The base uses reduced dairy, khoya/mawa, condensed milk, pistachio, and a small amount of stabiliser to mimic kulfi's slightly chewy, sticky texture while still spinning cleanly in a Ninja CREAMi.

## Ingredient table

Yield: one standard Ninja CREAMi pint, about 500-520 g base, filled to the MAX FILL line after chilling.

| Ingredient | Amount | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Whole milk | 220 ml / about 226 g | Dairy body and cooked-milk flavour |
| Heavy cream, 35% fat | 90 ml / about 90 g | Butterfat and richness |
| Evaporated milk, unsweetened | 100 g | Concentrated milk solids and kulfi density |
| Khoya/mawa, grated or crumbled | 45 g | Authentic cooked-dairy flavour and dense, slightly chewy body |
| Sweetened condensed milk | 55 g | Sweetness, milk solids, and softer spin |
| Granulated sugar | 20 g | Freezing-point control and sweetness balance |
| Skim milk powder | 14 g | Extra milk solids to reduce iciness |
| Fine sea salt | 0.8 g | Rounds sweetness and dairy flavour |
| Ground cardamom, freshly ground if possible | 1.2 g, from about 8-10 green pods | Main kulfi flavour |
| Saffron threads | 0.08 g, generous pinch | Optional but excellent |
| Rose water | 2 ml | Optional floral accent |
| Kewra water | 0.5-1 ml | Optional; very strong, use sparingly |
| Xanthan gum | 0.35 g | CREAMi-compatible stabiliser; reduces iciness and adds slight stickiness |
| Pistachios, finely chopped | 18 g | Mix-in and garnish |
| Almonds, finely chopped | 8 g | Optional mix-in and garnish |

## Equipment

- Ninja CREAMi with one standard pint container
- Small saucepan
- Whisk
- Fine sieve
- Digital scale, ideally accurate to 0.1 g for gum, cardamom, and saffron
- Blender or immersion blender
- Ice bath or shallow cooling container

## Method

1. **Infuse and reduce the dairy.** In a saucepan, combine the whole milk, heavy cream, evaporated milk, khoya/mawa, condensed milk, sugar, skim milk powder, salt, cardamom, and saffron. Whisk well. Heat over medium-low, stirring often, until steaming and lightly simmering. Do not boil hard.

2. **Cook for kulfi flavour.** Simmer gently for 8-10 minutes, scraping the bottom frequently, until the khoya dissolves and the mixture tastes cooked and nutty. This step is important: for kulfi texture and flavour, the base should taste like reduced milk, not plain sweet cream.

3. **Hydrate the stabiliser.** Remove from heat. Sprinkle in the xanthan gum while blending with an immersion blender, or transfer to a blender and blend for 20-30 seconds. Avoid dumping the gum in at once, or it may clump.

4. **Add floral accents carefully.** Stir in the rose water and kewra water after cooking. Do not cook them, as heat can make the aroma harsh or perfumey.

5. **Strain and adjust volume.** Strain through a fine sieve into a measuring jug. Cool slightly, then pour into the Ninja CREAMi pint. The base should reach the MAX FILL line. If it is short, add a small splash of whole milk. If it is over, reserve the excess; do not overfill.

6. **Chill before freezing.** Cool the pint uncovered in the refrigerator until no longer warm, then cover. For best texture, chill to below 5 degrees C before freezing.

7. **Freeze flat and solid.** Freeze the pint on a level surface for at least 24 hours. For dense kulfi-style texture, freeze fully solid; do not short-freeze.

8. **Spin in the Ninja CREAMi.** Remove the pint from the freezer. If the top has a large hump, level it slightly. Spin on **Gelato** if available; otherwise use **Ice Cream**.
   - For kulfi texture, avoid repeated unnecessary spins because they add more air and soften the base.
   - If crumbly after the first spin, add 10-15 ml cold whole milk and use **Re-spin** once.

9. **Add nuts.** Make a small well in the centre, add the chopped pistachios and almonds, and run **Mix-In**. For a denser kulfi feel, you may instead fold the nuts in by hand after spinning.

10. **Firm briefly before serving.** For the closest kulfi texture, return the spun pint to the freezer for 20-30 minutes before scooping. Serve in small dense scoops with extra pistachio.

## Science and troubleshooting

- **Butterfat target:** About 11-13%, rich but not so high that the base greases out during spinning.
- **Total solids:** Evaporated milk, khoya, condensed milk, and skim milk powder mimic reduced kulfi milk and reduce iciness in the CREAMi.
- **Sugar balance:** Condensed milk plus a little granulated sugar keeps the frozen base spinable without making it American-ice-cream sweet.
- **Stabiliser choice:** Xanthan gum controls free water, reduces iciness, and helps create the slightly sticky kulfi mouthfeel without requiring eggs or a custard base.
- **Khoya/mawa behaviour:** The 45 g khoya/mawa makes the spin denser and more kulfi-like. More than about 60 g can make the texture chalky unless the base is blended very thoroughly.
- **Cooked base:** A no-cook version will taste more like cardamom ice cream than kulfi. Cooking is the main flavour difference here.

Common failure modes and fixes:

- **Too icy:** Increase skim milk powder by 5 g, ensure the xanthan is fully blended, or cook the base 2-3 minutes longer.
- **Too soft after spinning:** Reduce added re-spin liquid, freeze 30 minutes after spinning, or reduce sugar by 5 g next time.
- **Greasy or buttery:** The cream level was too high or the base was overprocessed; reduce cream by 15-20 g and replace it with whole milk.
- **Cardamom bitter:** Use only green pod seeds, not husks, and avoid old pre-ground cardamom.
- **Rose tastes soapy:** Reduce rose water to 1 ml or omit it; kewra is also potent, so keep it under 1 ml.
- **Chalky:** Blend longer after adding milk powder and khoya, then strain before freezing.
